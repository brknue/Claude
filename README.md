# Claude

Things made with Claude.

**See them all on [plain](https://brknue.github.io/Claude/)** — open each project in the browser, download it, or read its code. How to add a project: [`plain/README.md`](plain/README.md).

- [`rookstorm/`](rookstorm/): chess site in 20 languages — play Stockfish 17 at any Elo (100–3200) and every chess variant vs the computer (incl. Crazyhouse), friends via link or online players, game review with accuracy, game rating and move badges, board editor, 3,800+ openings and endless rated puzzles; works offline. Accounts (Supabase) are built in and switch on once configured.
- [`molecule-lab/`](molecule-lab/): interactive chemistry playground (molecule builder, DNA lab, mitosis and meiosis animations).
- [`physica/`](physica/): virtual physics laboratory with 15 interactive labs (electrons, orbits, waves, chaos, gases, optics, fluids, quantum tunnelling, black holes and more).
- [`plain-wiki/`](plain-wiki/): paste any Wikipedia link and get the whole article rewritten so anyone can understand it (4 levels, 34 output languages, dark mode, 100 % local AI in the browser).
- [`youtube-to-pdf/`](youtube-to-pdf/): paste a YouTube link and download everything said in the video as a PDF (captions, optional timestamps, language picker).
- [`fluent-path/`](fluent-path/): offline language-learning site with a daily plan, spaced repetition and saved progress (Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, English; no build step, no external services).
- [`der-die-das/`](der-die-das/): learn the article of 5000 German nouns in short rounds with spaced repetition and memory aids (works on phone, tablet and computer, installable and offline).
- [`fuel-check/`](fuel-check/): type what you ate and did; works out calories, macros, tomorrow's fat/muscle change and feedback. No AI and no key: a built-in food table that tolerates typos, plus Open Food Facts for brands, barcodes and unknown foods (16 languages, every device).
- [`bio-genetik/`](bio-genetik/): Genetik-Lernseite für die Bio-Kursarbeit (Zellkern, Chromosomen, DNA, Replikation, Mitose, Proteinbiosynthese) mit 13 Animationen, Quiz, Karteikarten, Übungslabor und Probearbeit; 6 Sprachen, Fortschritt nur lokal gespeichert.
- [`lingua-local/`](lingua-local/): offline phrasebook and flashcards for six languages with a personal routine.
- [`mathe-klar/`](mathe-klar/): visual maths course for Realschule grades 5–10 (German), 77 animated lessons.
- [`verity/`](verity/): **Know**, an evidence-first fact checker that runs in the browser: finds claims and shows matching Wikipedia passages, optional on-device AI (no key).
- [`citypulse/`](citypulse/): small-towns explorer.
- [`gamepulse/`](gamepulse/): small game finder (not listed on plain).
- [`deep-dig/`](deep-dig/) and [`roblox-knowledge/`](roblox-knowledge/): Roblox game code and research notes (not web pages).
