# Simple Chess – Update notes

Big updates raise the first number (3.0 → 4.0). Small updates add to the big one (4.0 → 4.1).

---

## 5.0 – Learn, Play Coach and Advanced Stats
- **Lessons:** a new **Learn** tab with 17 short lessons on checkmates, tactics and endgames, each with 5 exercises on the board. Your progress is saved.
- **Courses:** 7 opening courses (Italian Game, Ruy Lopez, Queen's Gambit, Sicilian, Caro-Kann, French, King's Indian) with 5 real lines each. First with arrows, then from memory.
- **Play Coach:** in games against the computer the coach rates every move you make, shows the better move and lets you try again.
- **Advanced Stats:** wins, draws and losses as White and as Black, results per time control, rating history, winning streaks and your most played openings.

## 4.0 – Big boards on iPad, party upgrades and coming back to games
- Bigger boards on iPad, upright and sideways.
- The premove button only shows "✕" and the number of premoves.
- The Online menu shows how many players are searching in each time control.
- Party variants: takeback, infinite premoves and a promotion line on 4-player boards.
- 4-player chess shows what every player captured, in rows.
- Leave a game and come back: in games under 15 minutes you only lose if your clock runs out; in longer games you have 3 minutes.
- A phone that falls asleep or loses signal no longer ends the game.

## 3.1 – Smoother on phones
- Playing through a game in the analysis no longer scrolls the page.
- Tap an empty square to cancel premoves.
- Live games on profiles, with the time left before each game ends.

## 3.0 – Online play fixed on phones and iPads
- Online matchmaking, friend links and party tables work on phones and iPads, also on mobile data.
- On phones the "Next" buttons are right under the board.
- Play button in the analysis: watch the whole game, one move per second.

## 2.0 – Android app
- Simple Chess for Android as a free app, with its own icon.
- **Check for updates** in Settings finds new versions of the app and the website.

## 1.1 – Fully independent
- Simple Chess now has its own accounts, ratings, friends and online play, separate from Rookstorm.

## 1.0 – Simple Chess
- All the features of Rookstorm in a plain, hand-made look: white, black and grey only, no effects, no emojis.
