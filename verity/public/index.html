A SCORE WITH CONTEXT

What does the number mean?

The 1–100 score describes how strongly the evidence supports a claim. It is an AI assessment, not a probability or a guarantee of truth.

1–20Contradicted21–79Mixed / missing context80–100Supported

The overall score is the equal-weight mean of scored claims. Unverifiable claims stay unscored; coverage is shown separately. A high score with low coverage can still leave much unresolved.

Some things a link can’t tell us.

Private accounts, logins, paywalls, deleted posts and unavailable captions may block access. A shared video link often needs a pasted transcript or uploaded recording. Verity cannot read inside other apps, bypass access controls, prove a video is genuine or guarantee every claim is found. Evidence and AI assessments can be wrong.
