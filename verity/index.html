    Evidence report

    Optional: let an on-device AI read the evidence

    Runs entirely in your browser (no key, nothing sent to an AI company). Downloads a small model once (about 1 GB) and needs WebGPU: current Chrome or Edge, or Safari 26+. It only reads the passages shown below, so it can still be wrong.

    How it works

    1. Know splits the text into sentences and keeps those that state checkable facts (numbers, dates, names, “is/was/has”). You can edit the list.

    2. For each claim it searches Wikipedia and reads the best matching articles, then picks the sentences that share the most words and numbers with the claim.

    3. Labels: Numbers match = the passage talks about the same thing and contains the claim’s numbers. Numbers differ = the same topic with other numbers, read carefully. Related = on topic, but not decisive. Nothing clear = no good passage found. These are automatic text matches, not verdicts.

    Limits: Wikipedia can be incomplete or out of date, recent news is often missing, and word matching can miss meaning. Open the sources before you rely on anything.

    Privacy: no account, no tracking. Search words go to Wikipedia; links go to Jina Reader; the optional AI model is downloaded from Hugging Face. Nothing is stored except your theme and language.
