Physica

A virtual physics laboratory: 23 numerical simulations with real equations. Set parameters, run time series or parameter sweeps, fit models to the results and download every data point as a PDF report, CSV or JSON. Runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers; nothing is uploaded.

No labs match that search.

How it works: open a lab · adjust the controls · Experiment tab → record a time series or sweep a parameter · Data tab → plot any quantity against any other, fit a model (linear, power law, exponential, sinusoid, Gaussian…), read statistics and download the PDF report.

Keyboard inside a lab: Space pause · R reset · H hide panel · [ ] previous / next lab · Esc back to this list

On a phone: drag the handle at the bottom of a lab to open the controls; tap ⋯ for speed and quality.

All results are simulated. Models, equations and assumptions are listed in each lab’s Theory tab and in every report.
