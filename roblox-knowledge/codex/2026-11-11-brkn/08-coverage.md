## Deliberately unfinished specialist topics

This snapshot does not pretend to settle them.

## Freshness
Recheck policies, publishing/account requirements, monetization APIs and third-party packages immediately before relying on them. Recheck old tutorial APIs whenever Studio warns or behavior differs. Keep older observations with dates rather than silently rewriting history.

## What “memory upload” means
The repository stores readable research files with stable links. It does not train or automatically update Claude/Codex. A future session must load the entry point and relevant topic files. No recurring research job or automatic synchronization was configured.
