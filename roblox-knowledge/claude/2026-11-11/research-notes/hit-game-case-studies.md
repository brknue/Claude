# Roblox Hit Game Case Studies (2023-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-11. About 16 tool calls. CCU note: Roblox does not publish official all-time peaks per game. Figures come from Roblox's RTC/official X posts, Wikipedia (which cites press), or third-party trackers (Rolimons, RoStats, RoWatcher, RoVitals, Bloxguides). Third-party trackers often disagree, and several only cover readings since mid-2026, so their "peaks" are not all-time peaks. Treat every number below as approximate unless it is attributed to Roblox.

## Q1: Per-game case studies (peak CCU, visits, team, dev time, core loop, monetization, virality drivers)

### Takeaway
The 2025 mega-hits (Grow a Garden at about 22.3M CCU, Steal a Brainrot at about 25.4-25.8M CCU, 99 Nights in the Forest at about 14.2M CCU) were built cheaply and fast by tiny teams or solo teens. Studios (Splitting Point, DoBig) bought into them early. They then grew in step-changes driven by weekly timed updates and "admin abuse" events, with short-form video amplifying each step. In late 2026 the newest breakout is "Steal An Egg and Collect Rare Pets" (about 14.3M tracker peak, Sep 2026), a clone of the "steal" format.

### Cited Findings

**Grow a Garden (released March 26, 2025)**
- The creator is the anonymous 16-year-old "BMWLux". Per Jandel, the initial version took about **3 days** to build. Splitting Point Studios (led by Jandel) bought a share in April 2025 when the game had **about 1,000 CCU**. The original developer keeps 50%, and DoBig Studios later took a minority share. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Core loop: buy seeds with Sheckles, plant, harvest, sell, and buy rarer seeds. Crops keep growing while offline. Seed stock rotates, and weekly exclusive items must be claimed while online, which the article credits with helping retention. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Monetization: Robux to skip waiting, perks, the ability to steal other players' crops, and pets from loot boxes. DoBig has been criticized for excessive monetization. Jandel has declined to share revenue. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- The CCU staircase tracked each weekly update:

  | Date | Update | Peak CCU |
  |---|---|---|
  | May 18, 2025 | Blood Moon | ~5M |
  | May 31, 2025 | Bizzy Bees | 11.7M |
  | Jun 14, 2025 | Working Bees | 16M |
  | Jun 21, 2025 | Summer | 21.3M |
  | Aug 23, 2025 | "Admin war" with Steal a Brainrot | 22.3M |

  — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden). Roblox's RTC account gave the exact figure 22,346,725. — [RTC on X](https://x.com/Roblox_RTC/status/1959281995453706447)
- Roblox's newsroom said Grow a Garden set the Guinness record for most concurrently played video game at **21.6M**. — [Roblox Newsroom, Jun 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/06/roblox-infrastructure-supporting-record-breaking-games). This differs slightly from the 21.3M in Wikipedia and PocketGamer, likely because of measurement timing. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-grow-a-garden-hits-industry-record-213m-concurrent-users-surpassing-pubg-fortnite-and-more/)
- CCU fell to 7.6M within an hour of the June 21 peak, and the June 25 average was about 2.7M. The spikes are event-driven, not a steady state. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- It was the fastest Roblox game to reach 1B visits (33 days). By May 2026 it had 35.3B+ visits and about 10.9M favourites. About 35% of players are under 13 (per Jandel). It ranked #1 on Roblox's Top Earners chart by July 19, 2025. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Virality drivers: weekly updates and events (two event teams were formed in July 2025), a Travis Kelce celebrity event (Jul 26), the mock admin war, and coverage that NYT described as mostly short-form video. Rare items are traded for real money on eBay and Discord, against Roblox's terms of service. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Bot controversy: TD Cowen alleged bots inflated recommendation visibility, citing account-creation surges in the Philippines and Indonesia. It later retracted the manipulation claim. Roblox denied any inflation. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Halo effect: Roblox Q2 2025 DAU averaged 111.8M (+41% YoY), and 2025 bookings guidance was raised to $5.87-5.97B. A film adaptation is in development at Story Kitchen (announced Nov 2025). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- A sequel, "Grow a Garden 2", appears in RoStats surge data (+174,471 daily average on Jun 19, 2026). — [RoStats records](https://rostats.io/records)

**Steal a Brainrot (released May 16, 2025)**
- Developed by SpyderSammy ("Sammy") and owned by DoBig Studios. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). Rolimons lists the creator as "BRAZILIAN SPYDER". — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/109983668079237)

- Monetization: gear sold for in-game currency or Robux, plus purchasable server-admin abilities. Polygon criticized it as pay-to-win ("the best items are only available for cash"). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)

Rolimons lists an all-time peak of **25,836,222 on Oct 11, 2025**, which conflicts slightly with Wikipedia's 25.4M. — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/109983668079237)

### Inferences
- Development time for the 2025 mega-hits was days to weeks, not years. Grow a Garden's 3-day prototype is the only explicitly sourced figure. Studios with live-ops capacity (Splitting Point, DoBig) then bought equity at a tiny CCU (about 1K) and scaled content.
- Peak CCU on these hits is a scheduled-event phenomenon. Peaks are 3-8x the daily average (Grow a Garden: 21.3M peak vs about 2.7M average days later; Steal a Brainrot: 24-25M peaks vs about 1M average).
- In 2025 the biggest hits were idle/tycoon "collect, grow, steal" loops with timed stock and limited items. In 2026 the "Steal a ___" template keeps spawning new hits.

### Gaps
- Team sizes are largely undocumented. Only Grow a Garden (a solo creator plus the Splitting Point team; "two event teams" by July 2025) and 99 Nights (3 named developers) are sourced.
- Development times are not sourced for Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights, Dead Rails, Dress to Impress, Fisch or Rivals.
- No primary source was found for Blox Fruits, Rivals, Doors or Pet Simulator 99 all-time peaks. The figures above come from trackers or an older aggregator.
- The 99 Nights PC Gamer article returned 403, so its virality explanation (and any team or dev-time quotes) is unverified beyond the headline and snippet.
- Grace was not researched (no data found in this pass).
- Dress to Impress's all-time peak is uncertain: the range runs from 370K (Kotaku 2024) to an unverified 1.1M.
- Revenue figures for all games are absent apart from modeled tracker estimates.

## Q2: Common patterns across hits

### Takeaway
Across sources the recurring recipe is a simple, readable loop (collect, grow, steal, survive) with passive income and rarity tiers, delivered through weekly timed updates and admin-abuse events that create FOMO spikes. Short-form video, YouTubers and Roblox's own social channels amplify it, and the recommendation algorithm compounds it. Social and trading mechanics plus meme IP (brainrot) aid shareability. Most viral games still collapse, and sustained success depends on a live-ops cadence.

### Cited Findings
- **Weekly timed updates create CCU step-changes.** Each Grow a Garden record coincided with a named weekly update, and exclusive weekly items must be claimed online. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **Admin-abuse events and cross-game rivalries.** Steal a Brainrot's admin-abuse events gave out exclusive characters. The Grow a Garden vs Steal a Brainrot mock "admin war" on Aug 23, 2025 pushed Grow a Garden to 22.3M and Roblox to a platform record of about 47.3M CCU. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-peak-concurrent-user-count-hits-record-474m-as-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden-compete) (47.4M headline); [FRVR](https://frvr.com/blog/news/roblox-hit-its-highest-concurrent-player-count-ever-with-45-million-users-more-than-steams-all-time-peak) (45M headline; the platform-peak figures differ by source)
- **Meme culture and short-form video.** Steal a Brainrot used the Italian brainrot meme, and its TikTok and YouTube clips got tens of millions of views. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). NYT noted Grow a Garden coverage was mostly short-form video. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **Platform and creator amplification, with an algorithm feedback loop.** Dead Rails grew through Roblox's official TikTok, Instagram and X, then Flamingo and other YouTubers, then Top Trending placement. — [GameAnalytics](https://gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)
- **Celebrity and brand events as later-life spikes.** Grow a Garden had Travis Kelce. Steal a Brainrot's Bruno Mars concert drew 12.8M CCU. Dress to Impress had a Charli XCX tie-in. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/dress-to-impress-codes-roblox-dti-charli-xcx-1851622355)
- **Low-fidelity, fast prototypes.** Grow a Garden's first version took about 3 days. Critics called it "a prototype" (Kotaku) and "a pretty standard AFK game" (PCGamesN). — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **Early studio acquisition and co-ownership.** Splitting Point bought into Grow a Garden at about 1K CCU, and DoBig holds stakes in both Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot. Voldex acquired Brookhaven (February 2025). — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [Wikipedia List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- **Trading and real-money secondary markets.** Grow a Garden rare items trade on eBay and Discord, against Roblox's terms of service. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **Aggressive monetization:** paid stealing, loot-box pets, pay-to-win gear, and purchasable admin powers. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- **Survival and decay statistics.** These come from a single secondary source, RoWatcher:
  - About 80% of 2024 top-50 games were outside the top 200 by early 2026.
  - "Sustained success" means a peak above 50K CCU and still 20K+ at 12 months.
  - Recently updated games retain 3-4x more players at 6 months.
  - A regular, calendar-anchored seasonal-event cadence (at least quarterly) is the strongest single predictor of 12-month survival.
  - Simulators and tycoons decline fastest, while social and fashion games retain best.
  - Episodic horror (The Mimic, Doors-style) extends lifecycles by 12-24 months.

  — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/one-year-on-the-charts-what-really-happens-after-a-roblox-game-goes-viral)
- **Platform risks.** IP and licensing disputes, copycats on other platforms, exploit scripts, and bot-inflation allegations. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)

### Inferences
- The minimum viable formula appears to be: a 1-sentence loop, passive income plus rarity, a timed stock or event clock tied to real-world times (for example weekends), visible social interaction (stealing, trading, showing off), and a meme-friendly look. Ship ugly, then let a studio-grade live-ops team run weekly events.
- Copycat dynamics are strong. "Steal a ___" clones (Steal An Egg, Break and Steal an Egg) dominate the 2026 charts, so format-first iteration on a proven template is a viable strategy.
- Discovery is increasingly driven by Roblox's own channels plus creators. Being pick-up-able by the algorithm (high CCU-to-session conversion) matters more than marketing spend.

### Gaps
- No direct developer interviews explaining design decisions were fetched (99 Nights PC Gamer: 403; Dress to Impress devforum: not fetched).
- The RoWatcher decay statistics come from an undated secondary analysis without disclosed methodology. Treat them as indicative.
