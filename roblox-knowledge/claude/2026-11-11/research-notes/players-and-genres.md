# Roblox Player Base and What Players Like (2024-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-11. Latest reported quarter: Q3 2026 (reported Oct 29, 2026). Primary Roblox PDFs (shareholder letters/supplementals at s27.q4cdn.com) could not be text-extracted in this session, so many numbers come from news coverage of those documents; links to the primary PDFs are given where known for the report writer to verify.

## 1. Platform metrics: DAU, bookings, hours, age, region, device

### Takeaway
Roblox had an explosive 2025 (DAU +53% avg, bookings +55%, hours +69%), peaking at about 151.5M DAU in Q3 2025. Through 2026 DAU and hours have fallen year over year, to 138M DAU and about 34B hours in Q3 2026 (-9% and -15% YoY). Roblox attributes this to tough 2025 comparisons and seasonality, alongside mandatory age checks and discovery changes. The audience is aging up: 18+ users are 29% of age-checked DAU globally and 37% in the US, and they monetize better. Mobile remains about 80% of users.

### Cited Findings
**Quarterly trajectory**

- Q3 2025: 151.5M DAU (the all-time peak); 39.6B hours engaged, +91% YoY; about 2.8 hrs/day per user reported — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-had-151m-daily-active-users-in-q3-2025/); primary: [Q3 2025 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/Q3-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- Q3 2025 regional DAU (secondary table citing Roblox supplemental): APAC about 44.7M, US & Canada about 42.4M, Europe about 34.8M, Rest of World about 29.5M. On the call: US & Canada +32% YoY, APAC +108% YoY — [coopboardgames](https://coopboardgames.com/?p=27247) (secondary; verify against [Q3 2025 earnings release](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/roblox-fy2025-q3-earnings-release/))
- Q4 2025: 144M DAU, +69% YoY; 35B hours, +88% — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-daily-users-jump-69-to-record-144-million-in-q4-2025); bookings +63% YoY — [MetaTrader/Zacks](https://www.metatrader.com/es/news/1214403-rblx-q4-bookings-up-63-is-the-platform-entering-a-new-growth)
- Q4 2025 regional bookings: APAC +96% (Indonesia +700%, Japan +160%, India +110%); North America +41% and still the largest market — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-daily-users-jump-69-to-record-144-million-in-q4-2025); primary: [Q4 2025 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Q4-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- FY2025: revenue $4.9B (+36%), bookings $6.8B (+55%), 123.8B hours (+69%), average DAU 126.5M (+53%) — [GameDevReports summarizing Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025)
- Q1 2026: 132M DAU — [Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/) (secondary)
- Q2 2026: 123M DAU, +10% YoY but the third straight sequential decline. The CEO attributed this mainly to seasonality — [Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/). Revenue about $1.5B (+36%); bookings about $1.6B (+8%), at the low end of guidance. Roblox said age checks and discovery changes slowed new-user growth while retention stayed stable — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q2-2026-revenue-soars-36-to-15b-despite-bookings-miss); primary: [Q2 2026 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- Q2 2026: the top 10 games held "approximately 20% of hours," down from about 30% three years ago (CFO) — [Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/)
- Q3 2026: 138M DAU (-9% YoY, +12% QoQ, +55% on two years); nearly 34B hours (-15% YoY, +17% QoQ); bookings $1.697B (-12% YoY, above the high end of guidance, +50% or more on two years); revenue $1.572B (+16%); 30M average monthly unique payers (-16%); bookings per payer +5%; bookings per hour +4% globally and +2% in US & Canada. YoY DAU growth by country: France +12%, Germany +13%, Japan +33%, India +37%, Saudi Arabia +30% — [Gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion). One Quartr summary lists 29B hours, which conflicts with the other reports — [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/roblox-rblx-q3-2026_ozGBemPh)
- Q4 2026 guidance: bookings $1.89B-$2.0B (-10% to -15% YoY), assuming a seasonal DAU decline from Q3 — [Gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion)

**Age split**
- As of Jan 31, 2026, 45% of DAU were age-checked. Of those, 35% were under 13, 38% were 13-17 and 27% were 18+. Roblox said age-checked data shows a younger base than self-reported data — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-daily-users-jump-69-to-record-144-million-in-q4-2025)
- Q2 2026: 57% of global DAU age-checked; under-18 penetration 60% globally and 75% in the US. 18+ is 27% of age-checked users (about one-third in the US) — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q2-2026-revenue-soars-36-to-15b-despite-bookings-miss); [Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/)
- Q3 2026: 64% of global DAU age-checked (80% in Australia, 75% in the US and UK). 18+ is 29% of global age-checked DAU (up from 27% in Q2) and 37% of US age-checked DAU (up from 33%). US 18+ users monetize 50% or more above under-18 users — [Gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion)
- Open World data (June 2026): 18-24 year-olds are 34% of Roblox's audience, versus 29% across its dataset, and grew 50% YoY. Roblox's retention ranks in the top 5 of about 8,700 games, and it has the highest current-to-lapsed user ratio (about 174%) against Minecraft, Fortnite and GTA — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/data-shows-gamers-are-not-graduating-out-of-roblox-as-they-age-up/)

**Device split**
- About 80% of Roblox users are on mobile, contributing 46% of Robux revenue (Roblox annual report data) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/80-of-roblox-users-are-on-mobile-contributing-46-of-robux-revenue). Secondary sites cite the 2024 split as 80% mobile, 17% PC and 3% console — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/roblox-stats)
- GameAnalytics 2025: nearly half of players split time between PC and mobile; PC-only is the second-largest group, then mobile-only; console rose to about 2% of the player base — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/cn/reports/2025-roblox-report)
- Newzoo 2025: 80% access via mobile, but only 24% of players aged 10-65 play exclusively on mobile — [GameDevReports/Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)

### Inferences
- 2026 is a "digestion" year after the 2025 viral spike (Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot). DAU is still well above 2024 levels on a two-year basis, but the YoY trend turned negative in Q3 2026 and Q4 guidance implies continued YoY decline.
- The audience is skewing older (18+ share rising each quarter), and older users monetize better. This favors games designed for teens and adults, especially in the US and Europe.
- Growth is concentrated in APAC and emerging markets (India, Indonesia, Japan, Saudi Arabia). Localization and low-end mobile performance matter.
- Design for mobile first, but expect cross-play with PC to be common.

### Gaps
- No exact regional DAU split for 2026 quarters. The supplemental PDFs could not be parsed, so check the [Q2 2026 Supplemental](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Supplemental-Materials.pdf).
- Exact Q1/Q2 2026 hours were not found. Roblox no longer reports a clean under-13 / 13+ split for all users, only for age-checked users.
- No 2025-2026 official device split found; the 80/17/3 split is from 2024 data.
- The "17+" age band is not reported in earnings; Roblox reports 18+.

## 2. Genres dominating playtime and revenue

### Takeaway
Roleplay/hangout and simulation (including idle/collect/"brainrot" games) dominate visits. Steady anchors are Brookhaven RP (roleplay), Blox Fruits (anime adventure), RIVALS (shooter) and The Strongest Battlegrounds (anime fighting). 2025 hits were viral idle/collect games, and survival/horror (99 Nights in the Forest) was a breakout. Hits are getting bigger but shorter-lived, and the top-10 share of total hours is falling.

### Cited Findings
- Newzoo genre visits (Q1 2021-Q2 2025): Roleplay & Avatar Sim led at 116M daily visits; Simulation had 110M; Platformer and Survival had 70M each; Action had 59M — [GameDevReports/Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)
- Every month of 2025, Brookhaven RP and Blox Fruits were in the top 10. RIVALS and The Strongest Battlegrounds were in the top 10 for 11 of 12 months — [GameDevReports/Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025)
- 2025 breakouts: Grow a Garden had 12.3B visits in Q2; Steal a Brainrot had 26.4B visits in Q3; 99 Nights in the Forest passed 10B visits within months of launch — [GameDevReports/Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025)
- Concentration: the largest title's share of top-10 visits rose from 22% (Q1 2025) to 43% (Q4 2025), and three games held over 70% of top-10 visits by year-end. Grow a Garden fell out of the top 10 by December, and Steal a Brainrot lost about 30% of monthly visits from its August peak — [GameDevReports/Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025)
- Concurrency: Grow a Garden set a Guinness record with 21.6M CCU (July 2025); Steal a Brainrot was the first game past 25M CCU; the platform peaked at 47.4M CCU in Aug 2025 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-peak-concurrent-user-count-hits-record-474m-as-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden-compete)
- In May 2025, Grow a Garden led in both revenue ($12M) and playtime (1.2B hours). Brookhaven RP and Steal a Brainrot dominate playtime but earn less than their engagement would suggest — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-top-earning-games-in-june-2025/) (third-party estimates)
- Open World (2026): the most popular Roblox genres among its panel are shooter, sandbox and simulation, with horror, racing and survival also broadly liked — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/data-shows-gamers-are-not-graduating-out-of-roblox-as-they-age-up/)
- Competitive shooters are estimated at only 5-8% of CCU (third-party) — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-hit-12-million-concurrent-players-where-are-they-all-playing)
- Q2 2026: top-10 games' share of hours fell to about 20% from about 30% three years earlier — [Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/roblox-daily-active-users-fall-to-123-million-in-q2-2026/)

### Inferences
- The durable money and engagement genres are roleplay/social hangouts, anime action/fighting, and simulators/tycoons. Viral idle/collect/steal ("brainrot") games produce massive but short-lived spikes.
- Playtime and revenue diverge. Social roleplay earns less per hour than progression or collection games, which monetize via gamepasses and limited items.
- Long-tail hours are growing (falling top-10 share), so mid-tier niche games have room.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox share-of-hours or share-of-bookings by genre for 2025-2026 was found. Roblox's "Culture & Trends"/Replay reports were not fetched in detail.
- Revenue per game comes from third-party estimates only.
- Obby-specific and tycoon-specific share data was not found.

## 3. What players like and dislike

### Takeaway
Hard player-sentiment data is thin. Most evidence is academic or advocacy research critical of monetization (pay-to-progress, false urgency, gambling-like mechanics), plus Roblox-commissioned research that is favorable to ads. The strong retention and social co-play signals point to friends and social play as core draws. No quantitative survey of player dislikes was found.

### Cited Findings
- Penn State-linked qualitative study (23 interviews with players of UGC games, including Roblox) found "felt manipulation" and monetized mechanics as key predatory-monetization influences — [Penn State](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/more-than-just-microtransactions-predatory-monetization-in-user-g/)
- University of Sydney (Mar 2025) found children's Roblox spending concerning ("literally just child gambling") — [Univ. of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2025/03/24/literally-just-child-gambling-study-urges-swift-regulation-of-roblox.html). A 2026 follow-up found deceptive or misleading purchase design in 14 of 15 games studied, including countdown timers, limited-time offers, misleading pricing and pay-to-progress — [Univ. of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/05/17/inside-the-pay-to-play-world-of-roblox-targeting-children-.html)
- Fairplay's "Buying to Belong" report (Feb 2025) covers social pressure to buy avatar items — [Fairplay](https://fairplayforkids.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Buying-to-Belong.pdf)
- Roblox/Ipsos (Roblox-commissioned): 52% of users aged 13-34 say they pay more attention to ads on Roblox than elsewhere — [Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-roblox-announces-new-ad-research-and-measurement-partnerships-at-cannes-lions)
- A DevForum developer complaint says sponsored-ad traffic degrades engagement metrics used for discovery, so paid acquisition harms organic ranking — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)
- Avatar expression is a major activity: 274M daily avatar updates in 2025 — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-saw-274m-daily-avatar-updates-in-2025/)

### Inferences
- Aggressive monetization (timers, pay-to-progress, loot-style mechanics) is drawing research and regulatory scrutiny. Fair, cosmetic-leaning monetization lowers both reputational and policy risk.
- Social and identity features (avatars, friends, trading, roleplay) are central to why players stay.

### Gaps
- No Roblox-published or large-sample survey measuring player dislike of pay-to-win, grinding, update cadence or ads was found.
- Reddit and DevForum sentiment was not systematically summarized within the time budget.

## 4. Safety and age policy changes affecting audiences

### Takeaway
From late 2025 into 2026, Roblox made facial age estimation or ID checks mandatory for chat (global from January 2026). It limited chat to similar age bands, removed unrated experiences from public play, and gated "Restricted" (17+) content and private-space hangouts to verified adults. Roblox said these changes slowed new-user growth in 2026. They also reshape who can find mature-themed or social games.

### Cited Findings
- Age check (Facial Age Estimation via Persona, or ID) became required to chat. It started in December 2025 in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands and went global in early January 2026. Verified users chat within similar age bands unless they are Trusted Connections. Chat is off by default for under-9s without parental consent. Roblox reports a mean absolute error of 1.4 years for under-18s — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/01/roblox-age-checks-required-to-chat); [Roblox Newsroom Nov 2025](https://corp.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/age-check-requirement-to-chat-now-live-globally/4226101)
- Users have raised accuracy concerns about the facial checks — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Roblox-s-mandatory-facial-recognition-centric-age-verification-rolls-out-as-users-raise-accuracy-concerns.1168739.0.html)
- Unrated experiences are restricted to the developer and team only; secondary sources date this to Sept 30, 2025 — [Roblox Newsroom Aug 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/08/extending-roblox-policy-on-romantic-and-sexual-content); [Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-content-moderation-a-developer-s-survival-guide)
- Social hangouts with private spaces (bedrooms, bathrooms) are restricted to ID-verified 17+ users and are not shown in charts or recommendations to others — [Roblox Newsroom Aug 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/08/extending-roblox-policy-on-romantic-and-sexual-content)
- "Restricted" label: servers remove non-verified-17+ players, and the label cannot be changed back. From Nov 2025, under-18s no longer see Restricted experiences in search or recommendations, with a full block for under-18s announced for later — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updating-age-requirements-for-experiences-with-‘restricted’-content-maturity-label/3905863); [Content Maturity docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)
- Q2 2026: Roblox said age checks and discovery changes slowed new-user growth while retention was stable — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q2-2026-revenue-soars-36-to-15b-despite-bookings-miss)
- Infrastructure and trust & safety cost rose 24% YoY in Q3 2026, to $258M — [Gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion)

### Inferences
- Games that rely on open chat between strangers of mixed ages lost friction-free social reach. Games with built-in non-chat social mechanics (emotes, trading UIs, co-op tasks) are less affected.
- Games must get a content maturity label to be playable at all. Choosing Minimal/Mild maximizes the addressable audience, while Restricted cuts reach to verified adults.

### Gaps
- No quantified data on how much DAU was lost to age checks.
- Could not confirm whether the full under-18 block on Restricted experiences has taken effect.
