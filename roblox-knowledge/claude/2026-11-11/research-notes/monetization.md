# Roblox Monetization: Methods, Rates, Earnings Data, and Pricing Best Practices (as of Nov 2026)

## 1. DevEx rate and requirements, Robux-to-USD economics, marketplace fees, and Premium Payouts / Creator Rewards

### Takeaway
The standard DevEx rate is $0.0038 per earned Robux, up from $0.0035 on Sept 5, 2025. Since June 8, 2026, Robux spent by 18+ players in qualifying "novel" games converts at about $0.005396. Creators keep 70% of Robux spent on their own in-experience items. Premium Payouts (Engagement-Based Payouts) were retired in July 2025 and replaced by Creator Rewards. Roblox is also replacing DevEx with "Roblox Wallet" daily payouts for US creators aged 18+, starting late 2026. One unconfirmed report says US DevEx ends in mid-2027.

### Cited Findings
**DevEx rate**
- DevEx rose 8.5% from $0.0035 to $0.0038 per Robux, effective Sept 5, 2025, and applies only to Robux earned from then on. — [Roblox FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm); [Roblox RDC 2025 press release](https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/roblox-a-roblox-unveils-ai-monetization-and-performance-innovations-for-creators-20250905)
- A DevForum thread points out that 8.5% on $0.0035 is about $0.0037975, so Roblox rounded to the published $0.0038. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/incorrect-devex-rate-percentage-update-85-claim-is-not-accurate-%F0%9F%A5%80%E2%98%A0%EF%B8%8F/3920224/1)
- The 2026 18+ rate (announced around May 1, 2026, effective June 8):
  - Robux spent by 18+ players in "novel" games converts at $0.005396 per Robux, 42% above $0.0038. For example, 30,000 Robux pays about $162 instead of about $114.
  - To qualify, a game must be "novel" ("genre-defining", "deeper gameplay [and] distinct visuals") and use R15 avatars.
  - The rate covers passes, Robux subscriptions, select in-game items and private servers.
  - Source: [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=192104). Secondary coverage describes it as applying to "18+ U.S. users". — [gameaccountvalue summary of RDC 2026](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html)

**DevEx minimums and eligibility**
- The minimum earned Robux to cash out fell from 100,000 to 50,000 (Jan 2022), then to 30,000 (Jan 2023). — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- Developers must be 13 or older to join DevEx. — [Roblox Economic Impact Report, Sept 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-annual-economic-impact-report)

**Roblox Wallet and the DevEx transition**
- Roblox Wallet launches late 2026, run with Airwallex US:
  - Earnings are paid automatically every business day in USD.
  - Creators can transfer to a bank account whenever they want.
  - Creator Hub shows cash flow in real time, down to individual purchases.
  - It starts with eligible US creators aged 18+, and goes international in 2027.
  - A linked Roblox Card is planned for 2027.
  - Sources: [DevForum RDC26 announcements](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880); [gameaccountvalue](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html)
- "We'll transition away from DevEx in mid-2027 in the US. DevEx will remain longer in other regions." This comes from a user comment quoting Roblox, and is not in the official announcement text. — [DevForum RDC26 thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880)

**Robux economics**
- Average Robux selling price was about $0.01 per Robux in FY2025. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- Revenue split on virtual items:

  | Sale | Creator share |
  |---|---|
  | Items sold in the creator's own experience | 70% of Robux spent |
  | Marketplace items: general split | 30% creator / 40% distributor / 30% platform |
  | Marketplace items: minimum | at least 30% |

  Source: [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- App store fees: Apple and Google take up to 30% of user payments, and consoles take about 30%. In 2025, 29% of revenue came through the Apple App Store and 15% through Google Play. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)

**Premium Payouts replaced by Creator Rewards**
- Creator Rewards launched July 2025, replacing Engagement-Based Payouts, which paid by share of Premium-subscriber time. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- Creator Rewards also replaced the Creator Affiliate program. — [Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)
- Daily Engagement Rewards:
  - Pays 5 Robux per Active Spender per day, when the creator's game is one of the first three that player launches that day and they play 10+ minutes.
  - An Active Spender has made at least $9.99 of Qualifying Purchases in the past 60 days (Robux, Premium, or UGC subscriptions), and was not a new or reactivated user in that window.
  - All creators started earning on July 24, 2025.
  - Source: [Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)
- Audience Expansion Rewards:
  - Pays a 35% revenue share on a new or reactivated user's first $100 of Qualifying Purchases anywhere on Roblox, during their first 60 days.
  - The user must join through the creator's Share Link, a direct link, or a search for the game's name, and play 10+ minutes.
  - The game must average 100+ DAU over the 60 days after the join.
  - The creator account must be ID-verified.
  - Rewards carry a 60-day hold.
  - Source: [Creator Rewards docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)

**Roblox Plus (launched April 30, 2026; $4.99/month)**
- Subscriber discounts:
  - Subscribers get 10% off eligible purchases for the first two months, then 20%.
  - Roblox subsidizes the discount, so creators still earn the full amount.
  - That raises the effective creator share on a 100-Robux item from 70% to 78% (10% off) or 88% (20% off).
  - Source: [Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/roblox-plus)
- Sign-up bonus: 250 Robux per month for each subscriber's first three paid months, up to 750 Robux, when they subscribe through the game's prompt. — [Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/roblox-plus)
- Private servers: up to 100 Robux per subscriber who spends 60+ minutes in 30 days in their own paid private server, if it is among that subscriber's top 5. — [Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/roblox-plus)
- Transfers: creators get 10% of Robux transfers prompted in their game (10–500 Robux per transfer). — [Roblox Plus docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/roblox-plus)
- Price and launch date: [Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260410099173/en/Roblox-Announces-Roblox-Plus-a-New-Subscription-Plan-Offering-Exclusive-Platform-Benefits); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-plus/4567894)
- Plus incentives paid creators over 300M Robux in their first four months, through Aug 17, 2026. — [gameaccountvalue](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html)

### Inferences
- At the standard rate, 1,000 Robux spent on an in-game item works out to about 700 earned Robux, or about $2.66 in DevEx. That is roughly 27% of the ~$10 the player paid. The rest goes to app stores, Roblox's platform share, and the Robux-to-USD spread.
- At the 18+ novel-game rate, the same purchase pays about $3.78.
- Creator Rewards favors games that lots of paying players open first each day, and growth through Share Links.

### Gaps
- I could not reach the official DevEx docs page (404). Current KYC, tax, and 13+/18+ rules come from secondary sources and the 10-K.
- I did not find an official, primary confirmation of the mid-2027 US DevEx sunset.
- I did not confirm whether the Roblox Premium subscription itself was retired or renamed into Plus. The Plus docs only say Premium subscribers keep "the same Marketplace access."

## 2. How each monetization method works, revenue share, and best practices

### Takeaway
The core income is Robux sales of passes, developer products, private servers and subscriptions, at a 70% creator share. On top of that, Roblox has added several layers:
- Paid access (Robux or local currency)
- Ads: Rewarded Video, Immersive Ads, and pre-roll ads coming soon
- Creator Rewards and Roblox Plus incentives
- Managed Pricing: regional pricing plus A/B price optimization
- UGC and Marketplace commissions, plus Commerce
- Plugin and model sales in USD

### Cited Findings
**Methods listed in Roblox's docs**
- Roblox's monetization docs list:
  - Subscriptions (monthly)
  - Passes (one-time)
  - Developer products (repeatable, such as currency or potions)
  - Paid access (Robux or local currency)
  - Private servers (monthly Robux fee)
  - Immersive ads
  - Roblox Plus earnings
  - Catalog/UGC items (upload fee plus a commission; game owners also earn a commission on purchases from the avatar inspect menu or avatar editor)
  - Plugins and models (USD sales, minimum $4.99 and $2.99, 30-day escrow)
- The docs page does not state a percentage for most of these.
- Roblox does not record purchase data automatically, so store it yourself (DataStoreService).
- Source: [Roblox monetization docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization)

**Paid access in local currency**
- Since Jan 31, 2025, creators can sell paid access in local currency and earn "up to 70%". Roblox's own example: a $9.99 experience pays $4.99 (50%) per new purchase. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/earn-a-higher-revenue-share-for-999-paid-access-experiences/3294010)

**Managed Pricing: price optimization and regional pricing**
- Price optimization runs automatic A/B price tests on passes and developer products (not subscriptions):
  - It needs about 60,000 transactions in the past 30 days, and prices must be set by script through MarketplaceService.
  - New prices are applied only if total revenue goes up.
  - Tests re-run at least every 90 days and take about 3 weeks.
  - 2% of users stay on original prices as a holdout.
  - Source: [Price optimization docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/price-optimization)
- Regional pricing launched in April 2025. It lowers in-game item prices in countries with less spending power. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/04/22/roblox-regional-pricing-in-experience-items/)
- On the Q1 2025 earnings call, the CEO said differential Robux pricing "is really working". — [Q1 2025 call](https://www.alphaspread.com/security/nyse/rblx/investor-relations/earnings-call/q1-2025)
- Third-party claims of a 26% to 80% lift are unattributed. Do not rely on them. — [rolearn.dev](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/regional-pricing-international-markets)

**Ads**
- Rewarded Video: Roblox expected it to make up "up to 3%" of creator earnings early on, and aims for ads to grow most creators' revenue 5–10% long term. Brookhaven's creators earned five figures a month from it. Ads show only to users 13+. — [DevForum: More Creators Can Now Use Rewarded Video Ads](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-creators-can-now-use-rewarded-video-ads/3838678); [Ads RDC 2025 recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106)
- Pre-roll ads (coming soon): skippable video ads while a game loads, with no integration needed. Roblox shares the revenue; the split is undisclosed. Roblox Plus subscribers will not see them. — [RDC26 announcements](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880)
- Ads Manager "Earnings" campaigns, from the Oct 2, 2026 recap:
  - Over 1,200 games used them in beta.
  - Nearly two-thirds of games that reinvested up to 4% of earnings reached ROAS above 100%.
  - Example: Ink Game spent $250/day for a 277% return.
  - The feature is expanding to about 30,000 games.
  - Source: [DevForum Ads Manager RDC Recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790)

**Commerce and UGC**
- UAE creator @Cryptic_Desire passed 30M clothing orders. — [Economic Impact Report 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-annual-economic-impact-report)
- Creators can now sell emissive items on Marketplace. — [RDC26](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880)

**Roblox Everywhere (late 2026)**
- Creators can ship eligible games as standalone apps on mobile, PC and console, and on the web. No fees were disclosed. — [RDC26](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)

### Inferences
- If a game earns over 60k transactions a month, setting prices by script and turning on Managed Pricing is low effort and low risk, because Roblox only applies prices that raise total revenue.
- Rewarded Video looks like an add-on worth single-digit percentages of revenue, not a main income source.

### Gaps
- I found no official USD price tiers for in-experience subscriptions.
- I found no published CPM or revenue share for Immersive or Rewarded ads; third-party CPM claims are unverified.
- I found no physical-commerce (Shopify integration) revenue figures.

## 3. Real earnings data

### Takeaway
Creators earned $1.503B through DevEx in calendar 2025, up 63% from $922.8M in 2024. In the 12 months to June 2026 they earned about $1.7B, and over $5B since 2013. Earnings are extremely concentrated:
- Top 10 creators: about $65.7M each on average.
- Median DevEx creator: about $1,500 a year.

### Cited Findings
**Platform totals**
- DevEx fees were $1,503.1M in 2025 and $922.8M in 2024. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- Over 23,500 creators were paid in 2025, and 35,500+ were registered by year-end, 10,500+ of them new. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- RDC 2026 figures:
  - About $1.7B through DevEx in the 12 months to June 2026, up about 50%.
  - Over $5B since 2013.
  - 42,000+ DevEx participants, with a median of about $1,500.
  - Top 10 creators averaged $65.7M.
  - Sources: [gameaccountvalue](https://gameaccountvalue.com/news-roblox-2026-09-rdc-2026-creator-earnings.html); [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113585/robloxs-top-10-creators-averaged-dollars65-7-million-each-while-the-median-made-dollars1500-and-roblox-everywhere-is-the-fix/index.html); [Roblox newsroom RDC 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)

**2025 Economic Impact Report (calendar 2024 data, measured in Robux spent)**

|---|---|---|
| Top 10 developers | $33.9M | 450%+ |
| Top 100 developers | $6M | 500%+ |
| Top 1,000 developers | $820K | 570%+ |
| Median DevEx creator | $1,575 | — |

- Over $1B was paid from March 2024 to March 2025, up 31%+.
- Source: [Economic Impact Report](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-annual-economic-impact-report)

**Other data points**
- From the RDC 2025 release: the average Top-1,000 developer earned almost $1M, and the median DevEx creator earned $1,440 in the 12 months to June 30, 2025. — [RDC 2025 release](https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/roblox-a-roblox-unveils-ai-monetization-and-performance-innovations-for-creators-20250905)

  - 127M average DAU
  - About 1.8M daily paying users
  - $0.15 platform ABPDAU (average bookings per DAU)

  - 27-month average paying-user lifetime
  - About 2.7 hours per DAU per day
  - Source: [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)

**Top-game estimates (unofficial; Roblox does not publish per-game revenue)**

- Steal a Brainrot: tracker estimates range from $1.4M to $6.9M per month. — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/steal-a-brainrot); [rowatcher top 10](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)
- Roblox's own reporting credits both games as major revenue drivers. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-revenue-up-36-yy-as-grow-a-garden-and-steal-a-brainrot-drive-multiple-milestones)

**Benchmarks**
- Roblox gives no global benchmark. The Creator Dashboard compares payer conversion, ARPPU (average revenue per paying user) and ARPDAU (average revenue per daily active user) against similar games, for games with 100+ DAU. — [DevForum benchmarks](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285); [Monetization analytics docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)
- Anecdotal: one developer reported 1.2% payer conversion with an ARPPU of 40–60 Robux. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/very-bad-payer-conversion-rate-for-game-monetization-how-can-i-do-better/3302200)

### Inferences
- Platform-wide, about 1.8M of 127M DAU pay daily, roughly 1.4%. That fits the anecdotal ~1–2% daily payer conversion.
- As a rough check, a game averaging 10k DAU at about $0.01–0.02 take-home ARPDAU would earn about $36k–73k a year. This is unverified extrapolation.

### Gaps
- I found no current official count of developers earning more than $10k, $100k or $1M a year. Older reports had these figures, but I did not retrieve them.
- Top-100 and top-1,000 averages for the 2026 report were not retrieved; the TweakTown page was blocked.
- I found no reliable per-game revenue figures; tracker methods differ widely.

## 4. Pricing strategy, monetization design, and the paid random items (loot box) policy

### Takeaway
Roblox's official guidance stresses:
- Transparency, real discounts, and no false urgency.
- Calmer calls to action for minors.
- Serving both "tourists" (quick-effect items) and "locals" (long-term value such as battle passes).
- Social and limited-time items.
- Frequent themed updates.

Paid random items (eggs, gacha, spins, and keys or rerolls bought indirectly) must:
- Show every outcome's numerical odds before purchase.
- Pass a PolicyService eligibility check, with an alternative for restricted users.

### Cited Findings
**Roblox best practices**
- Be transparent and use chance-based monetization responsibly.
- Keep discounts genuine: no permanent "sales", and no discounts so short they pressure buyers.
- Avoid false urgency: no fake scarcity, and no countdown timers that are wrong or restart automatically.
- Use calmer language with minors ("View Item", not "GET IT NOW").
- Check eligibility with PolicyService before showing restricted products.
- Tourists like immediate-effect items; locals like long-term benefits such as battle passes.
- Limited-time items and trading drive social play.
- Update weekly, and no less than monthly, with themed updates.
- Let players earn new content.
- Source for all of the above: [Roblox monetization docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization)

**Paid random items policy**
- Show all outcomes and their numerical odds before Robux or Robux-bought currency is spent. This includes keys, reroll tokens and spin tickets.
- Odds must be percentages that add up to 100%, rounded to at least 4 significant digits, with a disclaimer if rounded.
- A long list can go in a pop-up, but it needs a labeled trigger ("Info" or "Details"); a lone (i) icon is not enough.
- If an outcome can only be won once, update the remaining odds for that player.
- Luck boosts, pity systems and rate-up items must state their numerical effect before purchase, and the odds shown must update while they are active.
- Random rewards that do not involve Robux or Robux-bought currency are exempt.
- Source: [Paid random items docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)

**PolicyService checks**
- If `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` is true for a player, you must do one of the following:
  - Offer a free, earnable path.
  - Use a fixed, disclosed order.
  - Sell specific items directly, priced at expected value.
  - Hide the item.
  - Block the purchase with a message.
  - Keep the player out of the area.
- If `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` is false, the player cannot trade paid items.
- Source: [Paid random items docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)
- Creators also disclose paid random items in the Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire. — [Paid random items docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/paid-random-items)
- The official page sets no age threshold. Restrictions vary by country and are enforced through the API. — [Paid random items docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)

### Inferences
- Gacha or egg systems should be built around PolicyService from day one, with a direct-purchase fallback priced at expected value, because some regions block paid random items.
- The 18+ DevEx premium and the "novel game" criteria reward deeper games that appeal to adults, not just aggressive monetization aimed at kids.

### Gaps
- I did not find sourced advice from top developers on specific patterns (starter packs, VIP, 2x passes, pay-to-win backlash) or specific price points. I found no reliable published sources for these, so they should not be presented as data-backed.
- No age rule for paid random items was found in the official docs.
