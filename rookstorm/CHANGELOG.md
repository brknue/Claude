# Rookstorm – Update notes

Big updates raise the first number (3.0 → 4.0). Small updates add to the big one (4.0 → 4.1).

---

## 8.0 – Learn, Play Coach and Advanced Stats
- **Lessons:** a new **Learn** tab with 17 short lessons on checkmates, tactics and endgames. Each lesson explains the idea and gives you 5 exercises on the board, from easy to harder. Your progress is saved.
- **Courses:** 7 opening courses (Italian Game, Ruy Lopez, Queen's Gambit, Sicilian, Caro-Kann, French, King's Indian) with 5 real lines each. Play every line with arrows first, then from memory.
- **Play Coach:** turn it on in a game against the computer. After every move the coach tells you if it was a book move, best, good, an inaccuracy, a mistake or a blunder. It shows you the better move and lets you take your move back and try again.
- **Advanced Stats:** on your profile and your friends' profiles. See wins, draws and losses as White and as Black, results per time control, rating history charts, your longest winning streak and your most played openings.

## 7.0 – Big boards on iPad, party upgrades and coming back to games
- **Bigger boards on iPad:** the board is now as big as the screen allows, like on lichess, upright and sideways.
- **Smaller premove button:** it only shows "✕" and the number of premoves.
- **Queue counts:** the Online menu shows how many players are searching in each time control right now.
- **Party variants:** takeback (everyone at the table has to agree; the computer always does), infinite premoves, and a promotion line on 4-player boards.
- **Captured pieces:** in 4-player chess you see what every player took, in the colour of the army it came from, neatly in rows.
- **Leave a game and come back:** you can leave a game against a person to look at other pages. A bar brings you back. In games under 15 minutes you only lose if your clock runs out. In longer games you have 3 minutes to come back.
- **Phone asleep or lost signal:** the game no longer ends. It reconnects by itself when you are back.

## 6.1 – Smoother on phones
- Playing through a game in the analysis no longer scrolls the page down.
- Tap an empty square to cancel your premoves, also on phones and iPads.
- **Live games on profiles:** see all running games of you and your friends, with the time left before the game ends.

## 6.0 – Online play fixed on phones and iPads
- Online matchmaking, friend links and party tables now work on phones and iPads, also on mobile data. If a direct connection is not possible, the game goes through our server instead.
- On phones the "Next" buttons in puzzles and the move buttons in the analysis are right under the board, no scrolling needed.
- **Play button in the analysis:** watch the whole game from start to end, one move per second.

## 5.7 – Simple look
