# CityPulse

Beautiful small towns from every continent, a filterable catalog, and quick links (OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia, GeoNames) to look up any other place on Earth.

- `index.html` — featured towns and search
- `catalog.html?group=Europe` — filterable list (`Europe`, `Asia`, `Africa`, `Americas`, `Oceania`)
- `track.html?city=slug` — one town, with a "Save to my list" button (saved only in your browser)

No live data, no third-party scripts or fonts, works on phones, tablets and computers.
